# Privacy Policy — SplitIt

*Last updated: 18 June 2026*

## 1. Data we collect

SplitIt only collects the data the app needs to work:

- **Email address and username** — to create and identify your account
- **Expenses and groups** — the amounts, descriptions and splits you enter
- **Receipt photos** — only if you choose to upload one

## 2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to:

- Let you sign in to your account
- Display and share your expenses with the members of your groups
- Improve the accuracy of receipt recognition (anonymously)

## 3. Sharing your data

We never sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Data is stored on secure servers (Supabase / AWS EU).

## 4. Data retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account at any time from within the app (Settings → Delete my account).

## 5. Security

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt). Communications are encrypted over HTTPS.
