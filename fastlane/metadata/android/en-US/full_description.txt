SCAN INSTEAD OF TYPING
Photograph the receipt. Splitit pulls out the items line by line. Everyone ticks what they had — and pays exactly that, not an average share. Scanning is free and unlimited.

MORE THAN ONE PAYER
Two or three people can front the same expense. Say who put in what; Splitit handles the rest.

THE FEWEST TRANSFERS POSSIBLE
Splitit won't tell you "you owe Marc £12, Léa £8 and Tom £4". It nets everything out and gives you the smallest set of transfers that clears the group.

REIMBURSEMENTS NOBODY ARGUES ABOUT
When someone pays you back, both sides confirm it. Until the other person agrees, the balance doesn't move. No more end-of-holiday "but I already paid you". Every reimbursement keeps its date, amount and payment method, and can be undone.

CHECK OUR ARITHMETIC
A panel unfolds the whole calculation: what each person fronted, their share, what they paid back, where they stand. The total comes to zero, and you can see it.

NOT EVERYONE NEEDS AN ACCOUNT
